About South Saint Paul Church Cleaning

Church cleaning in the South Saint Paul area for 22 years.

22 years is a long time to be in one business. We have cleaned through building renovations, pastoral transitions, capital campaigns, and the kind of slow institutional shifts that happen when a congregation grows or shrinks. None of that background is decorative. It means we understand how faith facilities operate over time, not just how they look on the first visit.

We are a service-area business. We come to you. We do not publish a street address because there is no storefront — our work is done inside your building, not ours. The contact information that matters is the phone and email that appear on every page of this site.

Our crews are insured, bonded, and carry workers' compensation coverage. A certificate of insurance is included with your written quote at no extra step. We do not subcontract your facility to a third party without your knowledge.

South Saint Paul has a particular character among the towns in this part of Dakota County. The older parishes along Concord Street and Southview Boulevard carry a different kind of history than the newer congregations in the subdivisions north of Marie Avenue. We have cleaned both kinds of space. The approach differs; the standard does not.

The Promise We Make in Writing

Before the first clean, you receive a written scope of work describing what will be done, what will not be done, which areas are included, what products will be used on which surfaces, and what the crew schedule is. That document is the standard we are held to. If we miss something in scope, we return within 24 hours at no charge. That is not a marketing claim — it is the written agreement.
