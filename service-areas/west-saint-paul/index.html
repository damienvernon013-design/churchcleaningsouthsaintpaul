Serving West Saint Paul Faith Facilities

West Saint Paul sits immediately south of Saint Paul along Robert Street, one of the metro's most active small-business corridors. Several congregations operate along or near the Robert Street spine, in buildings that date from the 1950s through 1980s and carry the maintenance challenges of that era — terrazzo floors, acoustic tile ceilings, and original wood pew finishes.

The city has a notably dense residential population relative to its 7-square-mile footprint, which means many congregations serve a walkable membership base with high mid-week programming activity. Fellowship halls see frequent use and require more intensive regular cleaning than facilities in lower-density communities.

The Thompson Avenue commercial corridor runs east-west through the city and connects several established faith communities. Drive time from South Saint Paul: approximately 8 minutes via Wentworth Avenue.
