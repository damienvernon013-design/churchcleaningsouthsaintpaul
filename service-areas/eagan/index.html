Serving Eagan Faith Facilities

Eagan is one of the largest cities in Dakota County with a population of approximately 70,000 and a significant corporate employment base including Thomson Reuters, Blue Cross Blue Shield, and several distribution and logistics operations. The faith facility landscape reflects this profile: newer, larger buildings constructed in the 1980s through 2000s, with contemporary sanctuary designs and large fellowship halls used for corporate-sized congregational events.

The Diffley Road and Pilot Knob Road corridors are particularly active for faith facility development. Several megachurches and large mainline congregations operate in Eagan, alongside smaller ethnic and immigrant congregations that have established footholds in the community over the last two decades.

Drive time from South Saint Paul: approximately 18 minutes via Interstate 494 or 20 minutes via Dodd Road south.
