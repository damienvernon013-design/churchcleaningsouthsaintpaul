Serving Inver Grove Heights Faith Facilities

Inver Grove Heights is Dakota County's largest city by land area at approximately 30 square miles, with development spread across distinct north, central, and south neighborhoods. Faith facilities in the north cluster near Concord Boulevard; those in the south are newer buildings constructed in the 1990s and 2000s with larger square footages and more modern mechanical systems.

The city borders South Saint Paul along 60th Street, making northern Inver Grove Heights one of the closest communities in the service area. Drive time from South Saint Paul: approximately 10 minutes via Robert Street to Concord Boulevard.

Several congregations in Inver Grove Heights operate school or childcare programs during the week, which means facilities that serve dual purposes and require cleaning schedules coordinated around both Sunday services and weekday programming.
