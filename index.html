Church Cleaning for South Saint Paul Congregations

South Saint Paul's congregations have deep roots here — from the established parishes along Southview Boulevard to the newer faith communities serving the city's growing northwest neighborhoods. Many of these buildings carry decades of history and require cleaning crews who understand how to work around worship schedules, mid-week programming, and the particular materials found in older sanctuaries.

We provide written scopes of work before a first clean, assign the same crew to your facility each visit, and inspect after every service. If something is not right, we return. Our response-time commitment: calls and emails received by 4:00pm receive a reply the same business day.

We have been in this business for 22 years. Most of the cleaning companies in this market have not. That longevity means we have cleaned through building renovations, capital campaigns, and changes in pastoral leadership — and we are still here.

What Facilities Administrators Ask Us

Do you work around Sunday services? Yes. We schedule cleaning for Saturday afternoons, Sunday evenings, or weekday mornings depending on your programming. We will not arrive during an active service.

Will I deal with different crews each visit? No. We assign a named crew to your building. If a crew member is unavailable, we notify you before the scheduled clean.

Can you handle a one-time post-event clean? Yes, though we are primarily a contracted service. A written scope covers what is done and what is not, so there are no misunderstandings about a holiday banquet or a funeral reception.

What makes you different from a general janitorial company? We clean faith facilities specifically. That means understanding which surfaces require gentler products, how to treat pew cushions and hymnals, and that a sanctuary is not a commercial office. The scope you receive reflects that.
